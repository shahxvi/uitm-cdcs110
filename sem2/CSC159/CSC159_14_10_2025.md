Types of Operand
- AX, BX, CX, DX (register)
  - MOV AX, BX (register oprand)
- 10 (immediate value)
  - MOV AL, 10
  - MOV BL, 5h (the number in BL must be hexadecimal)
    - BL = 0005h
  - MOV AX, 300 (Copy 012CH into AX)
    - AX = 012CH //300 in decimal terms
- VAL1 (variable or memory operand) //NOT VALI, BUT VAL1
  - MOV AX, VAL1
    - Copy whatever value in VAL1 to AX
  - MOV AX, slay
  - MOV AX, SLAY
  - Case-Sensitive
- `[200]` (memory location)
  - MOV  AX, `[200]` (Copy whatever in memmory address 200 to AX
  - Memory = RAM
  - Rare, but can come up in exam
