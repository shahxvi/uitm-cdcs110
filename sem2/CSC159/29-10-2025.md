# Sign-and-Magnitude Representation

Positive uses zero
Negative uses one

1 byte = 8 bit
1 byte = 00000000

The first at the front = Sign
The rest at the back = Magnitude

Example:
31 = 11111_2
+ 31 = 00011111
- 31 = 10011111
